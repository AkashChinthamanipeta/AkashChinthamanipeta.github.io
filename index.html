02· How one of them works

Here's how HelioNode works.

Quick walkthrough of how the soil sensor works end to end. Each node out in the field is independent — it powers itself from the sun and talks to a nearby hub. The hub does the rest. No subscriptions, no cloud bills, no apps to maintain.

SenseThree sensors keep watching what's happening in the soil and air.
ProcessThe little board on the node packages those readings together.
TransmitIt pings a hub nearby — no wifi or mobile data needed.
DeliverYou get a text, dashboard view, or smart-home update.
03· About me

Hi, I'm Akash.

I'm an engineer who can't stop building things. My day job pays the bills, but my real fun happens at my workbench — soldering tiny boards, debugging stubborn firmware, and trying to make affordable hardware that actually works in the real world.

What pulls me in is the gap between "this could exist" and "this exists and is in someone's garden right now." Most of my projects start with a small irritation — a plant that needed watering, a humidifier that didn't know I was in the room — and grow from there.

I design the hardware myself, write the software, and document the journey. Sometimes things break. Often they do. That's most of the fun.
